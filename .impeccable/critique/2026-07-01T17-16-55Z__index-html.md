## Overall Impression

A genuinely principled, human-authored public-health landing that beats the slop test and nails the emotional intent in its copy. The single biggest opportunity: it's built for a fearful, in-pain, low-digital-literacy phone user, yet the two things that matter most to that person — the movement-fear reassurance and the "how to sign up" step — are both hidden (behind a tap) or last (bottom of a long scroll).

## What's Working

1. **Anti-slop discipline is real and rare** — one eyebrow, chip/dot coding, letters-not-01/02. Serves the derivador/COVAM credibility audience without going cold.
2. **Progressive enhancement done properly** — `html.js` set synchronously; without JS the myth panels default open, timeline/team are static HTML, print force-opens panels. "Content + CTA work with no JS" is genuinely met.
3. **Conversion matches reality and lowers fear** — human, form-free CTA that preempts data anxiety; 3 steps map 1:1 to the real path; tel: link for immediate action.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Reassurance hidden behind a tap the fearful reader may not make.** The three myths (incl. "Si me duele al moverme, me estoy haciendo daño") are collapsed by default; the fear shows as a headline, the science is hidden. For a movement-fearful, low-literacy mobile user this inverts the emotional goal at the exact hinge. **Fix:** open the FIRST myth by default (`is-open` + `aria-expanded="true"`), add a visible "Ver respuesta" affordance beside the chevron. **Command:** `onboard` / `clarify`.
- **[P1] No wayfinding on a long 9-section page; sign-up is dead last.** Only a top skip-link and a bottom "↑ volver". **Fix:** sticky "Apuntarse" anchor to `#apuntarse` and/or a compact 4-item in-page nav after the hero; keep minimal. **Command:** `layout` / `adapt`.
- **[P1] The 16-session timeline is the heaviest cognitive object, unstructured.** 16 similar cards in one flat scroll; duplicates the "4 etapas" section without connecting. **Fix:** group by month or by Bloque A–D (tie marker color to bloque), and/or a "Ver las 16 sesiones" disclosure collapsed on mobile with static fallback. **Command:** `distill` / `layout`.
- **[P2] AA contrast edges + hidden legend.** Terracota tagline (#C2410C) and `--texto-suave` lead on the hero gradient, and amber-on-blue / green-brume chips, need verified ≥4.5:1; the timeline dot legend is `aria-hidden`. WCAG 2.2 AA is a hard target. **Fix:** verify/adjust contrasts; unhide the legend. **Command:** `audit` / `harden`.
- **[P2] Emoji as primary iconography.** 🗓️🕕👥💶 / 🧠🌿🚶🤝 / 🌍 render inconsistently on the barrio's older Android fleet and lean template. The page already has a clean inline-SVG idiom (checkmark, chevron, avatars). **Fix:** swap functional emoji to SVG. **Command:** `shape` / `polish`.
- **[P3] Untracked co-brand + minor motion drift.** Footer `#7FC0E4`/`#4FD0C0` not in DESIGN.md; `transition: padding` on the accordion. **Fix:** add the two footer colors as tokens (or reuse declared ones); optionally drop the padding transition. **Command:** `document` (tokens) / `polish`.

## Minor Observations

- `.specs` uses `role="list"`/`role="listitem"` on divs where `<ul>` is more robust.
- `.hero p.lead` at 1.05rem is below the 18px base — arguably the paragraph you'd most want at full size for this audience; several secondary texts drop to 0.78–0.95rem.
- "4 etapas" and the timeline overlap; timeline markers could inherit bloque colors to connect them.
- Open Graph present but no `og:image` — WhatsApp (the likely boca-a-boca channel) will show a text-only preview. Worth adding.
- `scroll-behavior: smooth` correctly disabled under reduced-motion.

## Questions to Consider

1. Should the sign-up ask appear in the hero, not just the footer? The page's whole job is "get them to mention it at their next appointment," yet that instruction is last.
2. Is the accordion the right pattern for the single most therapeutic content on the page? The myths *are* the treatment message — hiding the reassurance optimizes tidiness over the emotional goal.
3. Do the emoji quietly undercut the "serious public health with barrio warmth" positioning, given the page already proves it can do warm, device-robust SVG iconography?
